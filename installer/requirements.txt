Audiobook Forge turns ebooks (EPUB, PDF, Word, text, Markdown, web pages) into audiobooks with
chapters, using the Kokoro voice on your graphics card.

What it needs
- Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
- An AMD Radeon RX 9000 series graphics card (RX 9070 XT, RX 9070, RX 9060 XT) with a recent
  AMD Software: Adrenalin Edition driver. The voice runs on that card through AMD ROCm.
  On other graphics cards it still works, but on the processor, which is many times slower.
- About 7 GB of disk space for the program, plus room for the books you convert
  (about 0.4 GB per hour of audio while converting).
- An internet connection the first time a voice is used, to download it (a few MB each).

Install it in a folder whose path has no spaces (the default, C:\Audiobook-Forge, is fine).
Finished audiobooks are saved in the "output" folder inside it unless you choose another folder.
